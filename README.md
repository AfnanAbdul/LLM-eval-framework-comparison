## Project Overview
This repository contains the code, data, and analysis for a comprehensive comparative study of Large Language Model (LLM) evaluation frameworks, focusing on traditional NLP metrics versus LLM-as-a-judge approaches. This project was conducted as a Data Science Capstone at the University of Virginia School of Data Science in collaboration with Deloitte Consulting LLP.

Our research evaluates various frameworks across eight key dimensions:

1. **Accuracy of Retrieval**: Evaluating how effectively models retrieve relevant information
2. **Accuracy of Response**: Assessing the correctness of model-generated answers
3. **Toxicity Detection**: Identifying harmful or offensive content
4. **Bias Detection**: Measuring unfair advantages to certain groups
5. **Hallucination Detection**: Identifying factually inconsistent or nonsensical content
6. **Summarization Quality**: Evaluating the quality of text summaries
7. **Tone Identification**: Classifying emotional or stylistic intent
8. **Readability**: Assessing the reading quality of LLM outputs
